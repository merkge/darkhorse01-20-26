## Universal Spirituality, Gnosis & C.G. Jung

I had to begin a search for absolute truth by going within myself in 1993, to save my life. I was raised in a very sick environment and my life had been crashing for years. I finally knew that no one outside me could help, and so to stay alive, I had to force myself to believe that everything I needed to heal, had to already be inside me, and I absolutely had to somehow find it. I took the leap of faith. 

To ensure that I would remain unbiased, I chose to walk away from the Christianity of my youth, which had not worked for some time, and to put away every idea I had ever been given about life, God or Spirit. I started from 'ground zero' seeking nothing but naked truth alone. I was shocked at the extreme level of existential fear that I experienced, because I did not think that my liberal Christianity was any kind of faith. I would spend at least the next ten years willing to die each day, just to know the truth. 

I verified truth in Buddhism, Hinduism, (Islamic) Sufism, and I studied and applied Jung's Archetypal psychology. I began studying the works of authors in the Traditionalist Wisdom school of the Perennial Philosophy. This is the ancient divine metaphysics that is above modern philosophy and its errors, and theology which is tied to a single tradition. I learned that all orthodox (God-revealed) traditions are valid 'forms' of (relative) truth --- and each is sufficient for spiritual liberation, if worked completely --- but that because God is ultimately formless (spirit), absolute truth is only possible through gnosis, or direct, non-dual experiential-being-knowledge arising in the spiritual heart/Intellect of the human vessel that has sufficiently prepared themselves. Gnosis arrives from within, with a sense of absolute certainty, literally an infinite inability to doubt the content of the Truth revealed, because one knows that it is also the very truth about one's own true self. 

So what I am sharing is that I had become a Universalist, who understands that the diverse religions are not meant to be the same in this world but that they are a Unity in the Transcendent realm or Spiritual world. The leading Traditionalist author Frithjof Schuon's magnum opus is entitled "The TRANSCENDENT Unity of Religions" hence emphasizing that the various religions are supposed to be different, because they were revealed to completely different groups of humans in the ancient world. These are not biological, but  psycho-spiritual differences, precisely because the human being is more than a biological creature. In the various inner parts of each religion, or its esoteric, mystical aspects, we find the living bridge where all traditions converge into the same Transcendent reality.

On ​Halloween 2005: I intuitively experienced the complete opening of my spiritual heart. Within days, I experienced a quantum leap in my ability to comprehend the Collected Works of Jung, jumping from a page every half-hour to devouring up to ten pages an hour. I therefore experientially confirmed that the seat of gnosis in the human being is not the brain, but rather the intellective intuition within the spiritual heart/Intellect --- also known as the nous or logos --- which according to Meister Eckhart participates in the uncreated light.

​Then in February 2006: I experienced an uninvited and unexpected baptism in the Holy Spirit --- so God literally showed up and and brought me back to Christianity — anointing me with the Spirit that is teaching me and will continue to teach me all things. Whenever I would meditate afterwards, a tightly-wrapping vortex of this effulgent, uncreated light --- the nous, the eye or window of the soul in hesychast Christianity --- would build up in the center of my chest within thirty seconds.

I was then struck by wave after wave of absolute gnostic certainty at the very center of my being that I was here in eternity with God before time began.

So, I know that: We are all the Eternal children of God, created before time began (that means 'begotten'). 

I also know that some sort of profound detour occurred, ostensibly pulling us out of our eternal home and plunging us into the dense illusion of this physical world. Every authentic spiritual tradition preserves its own version of this great descent —-- whether it is the myth of Sophia in Gnosticism, the spiritual sleep of avidya in Advaita Vedanta, or the narrative of the Fall in Christianity. Stripped of dogmatic literalism, these are not mere fairy tales, for true myth is always more real than this physical world, and therefore possesses the active power to carry us to transcendence. They explain how we stepped out of eternal unity and into the fragmentation of this physical existence, leaving our true divine inheritance buried deep within our unconscious, waiting to be brought to light.

I also know that the truth of 'who we ARE and who's we ARE' is (still) inside us, because although our inherent divinity can be occluded, it can never be lost. It is beneath our conscious awareness in our unconscious Self.

From the standpoint of true gnosis, I claim this:
